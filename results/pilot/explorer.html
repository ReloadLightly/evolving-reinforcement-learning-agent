Descriptive method summaries
MethodSelected seedsMean final returnMean raw AUC

Final return measures the learner at exactly 50,000 training transitions. Raw AUC summarizes its entire measured learning curve, including initialization and warmup: trapezoidal integral divided by 50,000 steps. Its units are native episode return. A method can finish better yet have lower AUC if it improved later. Neither metric is normalized here, and raw values should not be averaged across these two tasks.

Changing a filter changes this display, not the experiment or analysis plan. Selecting a favorable seed does not establish a method advantage. The five evaluation episodes are repeated evaluations of one trained agent; they are not five independent training replications.
